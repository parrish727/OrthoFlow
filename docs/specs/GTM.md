## 1. Executive Summary

OrthoFlow AI is a production-ready, AI-native practice management system targeting orthodontic and dental practices. The platform is live, processing real clinical workflows, and differentiated by embedded AI that directly recovers revenue through automated denial analysis and appeal generation.

The go-to-market strategy targets small-to-mid-size orthodontic practices (2–5 providers) as the initial beachhead, expanding to general dentistry once product-market fit is validated and denial recovery data proves ROI. Distribution starts with pilot practices in the Midwest, scales regionally via conference presence and referral incentives, and reaches national coverage through strategic partnerships with DSO groups and dental suppliers.

Revenue model: SaaS monthly subscription per practice, three tiers based on provider count and AI feature access. Target: 50 practices at $15K MRR within 18 months of GTM launch.

## 4. Pricing Strategy

### Model

Monthly SaaS subscription per practice. No per-provider upcharge on base features. Annual contracts get 2 months free.

### Tiers

| Tier | Monthly Price | Includes | Target |
|------|--------------|----------|--------|
| **Essentials** | $499/mo | Scheduling, patient records, charting, basic claims, patient portal (up to 3 providers) | New/small practices transitioning from paper or basic systems |
| **Professional** | $899/mo | Everything in Essentials + AI denial review, AI appeal generation, ERA auto-posting, denial analytics, communications (up to 5 providers) | Core ICP — practices losing revenue to denials |
| **Enterprise** | $1,499/mo | Everything in Professional + multi-location, unlimited providers, API access, priority support, custom reporting, dedicated success manager | Growing practices and small DSOs |

### Add-Ons

| Add-On | Price |
|--------|-------|
| Additional providers (beyond tier limit) | $99/mo per provider |
| Data migration from legacy PMS | $2,500 one-time |
| Custom integration development | $150/hr |
| On-site training (travel + per diem) | $2,000/day |

### Pricing Rationale

- **$499 Essentials** undercuts Ortho2 ($700–$1,200/mo) and Cloud 9 ($600–$1,000/mo) while providing equivalent base functionality.
- **$899 Professional** pays for itself if it recovers even 2 denied claims per month (average ortho claim: $2,000–$6,000).
- **$1,499 Enterprise** positions below enterprise competitors ($2,000–$5,000/mo) while offering AI features they lack entirely.

## 9. Competitive Moat

### Network Effects (AI Improves With Scale)

Every claim processed through OrthoFlow trains the denial prediction model. As more practices join:

- Denial pattern analysis becomes more accurate per payer
- Success probability scoring improves with more outcomes data
- Appeal letter quality increases with more winning examples
- Payer-specific strategies refine automatically

A practice joining OrthoFlow today benefits from every denial ever processed by every other practice on the network (anonymized, aggregated). This advantage compounds over time and is not replicable by competitors starting from zero.

### Patient Portal Stickiness (MyOrthoChart)

Once patients are using MyOrthoChart for:

- Appointment management
- Secure messaging with their provider
- Treatment progress tracking
- Form completion and consent
- Billing and payment

The switching cost for the practice becomes enormous. Patients don't want a new login, new app, new workflow. Practices that switch away from OrthoFlow face patient backlash — the same dynamic that keeps hospitals on Epic.

### Data Moat

- 6+ years of audit trail data creates compliance dependency
- Historical denial patterns are practice-specific institutional knowledge
- Treatment outcome data enables predictive analytics competitors can't offer without equivalent history

### Operational Moat

- Self-hosted infrastructure means no cloud cost scaling surprises
- AI powered by Anthropic Claude — highest quality, no OpenAI dependency
- Stedi clearinghouse integration is a non-trivial engineering investment competitors must replicate
- HIPAA-compliant architecture built from day one, not bolted on

### Black-Owned Business Advantage

- Eligible for diversity supplier programs at DSOs and hospital-affiliated dental groups
- Resonates with practices serving diverse communities
- Access to minority business grants, accelerators, and procurement set-asides
- Authentic differentiation in a market dominated by PE-owned incumbents
