Reminders at 30 · 60 · 90 days past due

Real due dates

Each patient's payment plan has due dates on the Ledger. Late means days past a missed due date, not days since a charge.

Pay link by text

Each reminder texts a secure link that opens MyOrthoChart on the Billing page.

Automatic or manual

The doctor chooses at setup. Only patients who agreed to texts are contacted, only in office hours. Orthodontic balances only.
